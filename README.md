# Hi, I'm Riccardo di Silvio

Mathematics with Statistics undergraduate at the University of Surrey, working toward quantitative finance and applied mathematics research. I split my time between rigorous maths and the code that makes it run.

- 🎓 Final year. Dissertation on the Dirac equation (special relativity → spinors → relativistic hydrogen atom, with a C++ numerical solver)
- 📄 Co-authoring a paper on the Kelly Criterion and Shannon information theory, targeting arXiv
- 🔭 Founder of [L² Research](https://github.com/L2-Research), an independent student-led quantitative research group
- 🌱 Currently building [Math of ML](https://github.com/rickdisi/math-of-ml) — linear algebra through to a neural network, proved from first principles and implemented from scratch in C++17
- 💬 Interested in mathematical finance and computational science

## Featured Projects

**[math-of-ml](https://github.com/rickdisi/math-of-ml)**
An expository paper paired with a from-scratch C++17 implementation with no external libraries. Builds rigorously from linear algebra, calculus, statistics to neural networks.

**Kelly Criterion & Information Theory**
Research establishing the Kelly–Shannon equality, connecting optimal growth-rate betting to Shannon mutual information. In progress, targeting arXiv submission.
