## Hi, I'm Riccardo

Mathematics with Statistics undergraduate at the University of Surrey, working toward applied mathematics research. Interested in Modelling, Computing and Finance.

- Final year. Dissertation on Special Relativity and the Dirac equation (special relativity → spinors → relativistic hydrogen atom)
- Founder of [L² Research](https://github.com/L2-Research), an independent student-led quantitative research group
- Currently building a [Riemann–Siegel zero finder](https://github.com/rickdisi/riemann-siegel-zeroes): Zeros of the Riemann zeta function on the critical line, in C++
- Built [Math of ML](https://github.com/rickdisi/math-of-ml): Linear algebra through to a neural network, proved from first principles and implemented from scratch in C++
- Co-authoring a paper on the Kelly Criterion and Shannon information theory, targeting arXiv
